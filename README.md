**Velora — Customizable Mouse & Controller Macro Menu**

Velora is a Windows app with adjustable recoil controls, saved profiles, operator-based settings, rapid fire, Quick Peek, configurable hotkeys, and automatic updates.

- **Recoil settings:** Adjust downward pull, sideways pull, and ramp-up timing.
- **Saved profiles:** Keep separate mouse and controller configurations.
- **Flexible interface:** Use controller, mouse, or combined views.
- **License access:** Choose from the durations listed in the store.

**Current R6 compatibility:** Mouse recoil is affected and may be delayed or fail. There is **no ETA for a fix**. Controller recoil is working in our testing.

Compatibility with every game is not guaranteed.
